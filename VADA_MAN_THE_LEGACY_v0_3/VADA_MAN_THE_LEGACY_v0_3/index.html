01 / The premise

Every legend starts somewhere.

A dark-action comedy built from classroom-scale conflicts and exaggerated comic-book mythology.

At the centre is Kuhane, a student who sees himself as observant, intelligent and repeatedly misunderstood. His habit of listening in, collecting information and seeking favours strains the patience of the people around him.

A rotten-vada incident gives him a nickname. A confrontation turns a once-tolerated friendship into a fractured group. The backbenchers become a force of their own, while the Vada Man identity transforms everyday tensions into larger-than-life comic-book mythology.

The series moves between classroom comedy, rivalries, secrets, flashbacks and a darker final chapter. The characters and events are fictionalised for this story universe.

The central figure

Kuhane / Vada Man

Self-image versus outside perception
Open dossier +
Kuhane believes he is observant, clever and unfairly treated. His information-seeking habits and one-sided requests create friction. Vada Man is the exaggerated superhero identity built around the nickname—not a literal real-world identity.
The six-member crew

Backbench Syndicate

A group with a breaking point
Open dossier +
The six backbenchers were not enemies from the beginning. They initially tolerated Kuhane, but repeated interference and the confrontation eventually fractured the group. Their dynamics include humour, arguments and loyalty.
The running subplot

Ashvanth

A one-sided attachment gimmick

Ashvanth belongs to a teasing subplot built around a one-sided attachment. It is played as a comedic running gag, not a mutual romance.
